# Checked and set aside

Each of these looked promising and failed on cost, on recent law changes, or because the money goes to someone else.

| Idea | Why not now | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solar lease on farmland | LD 1777 (June 2025) ended net energy billing for new front-of-meter projects after 2025 and cut compensation for existing ones by about 20%. Since April 2025, solar projects of 5 acres or more on high-value farmland need a DACF permit, and those of 20 acres or more may owe compensation fees (DACF ch. 575). Developer demand is weak. **Changed 2025.** Revisit once the successor program is approved. | [BDN](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2025/07/09/politics/state-politics/maine-net-energy-billing-solar-subsidies-eliminated/), [GEO](https://www.maine.gov/meopa/sites/maine.gov.meopa/files/inline-files/2025-06-05%20LD%201777%20Information%20Sheet_0.pdf), [Maine Public](https://www.mainepublic.org/climate/2025-04-23/maine-approves-rules-to-restrict-solar-development-on-farmland), [DACF solar on farmland permits](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/ard/solar/solar-hval.shtml) |
| Septage hauling | LD 1911 (2022) bans spreading sludge on land because of PFAS, and no new septage land-application licenses are issued. A pump truck alone is over budget. | [NACWA](https://www.nacwa.org/news-publications/news-detail/2022/04/20/maine-legislature-passes-bill-prohibiting-land-application-of-biosolids-governor-expected-to-sign), [DEP septage](https://www.maine.gov/dep/waste/solidwaste/septage.html) |
| E-waste recycling | Manufacturers reimburse only DEP-approved consolidators (up to 10 a year; 5 approved for 2025), and collection is mostly municipal. | [DEP e-waste](https://www.maine.gov/dep/waste/ewaste/comply.html) |
| Packaging EPR | Payments go to municipalities, starting about October 2027, and the stewardship organization's selection has slipped. Worth watching for subcontract work. | [Waste Dive](https://www.wastedive.com/news/maine-extended-producer-responsibility-packaging-timeline/819817/) |
| Mattress recycling | The 2017 mattress stewardship bill was vetoed and the veto held. No mattress EPR program exists. | [LD 349 history](https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Meta/LegHist/128/lh128-LD-0349.pdf) |
| Scrap tires | No tire EPR. Hauling needs a DEP transporter license and manifests; low margin, and stockpiles become a liability. | [38 M.R.S. §1316-M](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/38/title38sec1316-M.html) |
| Composting for tipping fees | A farm can compost without a DEP permit within limits: 5 to 60 cu yd of food residuals per 30 days, or any amount if 70% is used on the farm. Earning tipping fees would need permitted volumes and food-waste contracts. Low priority. | [06-096 CMR ch. 410 via ILSR](https://ilsr.org/article/composting-for-community/maine) |
