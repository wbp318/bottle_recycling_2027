# Snow plowing contracts

**Rank 5.** Winter income from equipment the farm may already own.

## What it is

Towns, school districts (MSAD/RSU), and commercial lots put plowing and sanding out to bid, often on multi-year contracts. Recent examples:

- [Liberty, 2026 bid](https://libertymaine.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/snow-plowing-bid-contract-and-specs-2026.pdf)
- [Georgetown, 2025](https://georgetownme.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Snowplowing-municipal-areas-2025.pdf)
- [Cranberry Isles, 3-year contract](https://www.cranberryisles-me.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/GCI-3-Year-Plowing-Contract.pdf)

## Requirements

There's no state plowing license. Town contracts typically require:

- $2M per occurrence / $4M aggregate liability insurance
- The town named as additional insured
- Workers' comp, or a valid exemption

## Startup cost (est.): $8k–20k

A plow and sander for an existing truck. A tractor with a blower can serve driveways.

## Risks

- Snowfall varies; a seasonal (flat) contract cushions that, while per-push billing doesn't
- Insurance cost
- The partner is on call through every storm

## Who does what

| Maine partner | Remote partner |
| --- | --- |

## How it fits

It's busiest in winter, when the campsites earn nothing. Plowing the farm's own road for the redemption center and storage lot is part of the job anyway.
