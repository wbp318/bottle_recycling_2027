# Firewood

**Rank 4.** Good if the farm has a woodlot. Quarantine rules give in-state wood an edge.

## Rules that matter

**Emerald ash borer quarantine (changing in 2026).** All of Kennebec, Knox, Lincoln and Waldo counties are inside the quarantine area, which restricts moving hardwood firewood and ash out of it. A new map came out in April 2026. In summer 2026 DACF **proposed repealing the quarantine** after the beetle turned up in Benedicta, nearly joining the northern and southern infestations. Hearings ran through August 28, 2026 ([Maine Forest Service EAB page](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/mfs/forest_health/invasive_threats/eab/index.shtml); [Maine Public, July 2026](https://www.mainepublic.org/climate/2026-07-20/maine-wants-to-end-emerald-ash-borer-quarantine); [DACF repeal notice](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/MEDACF/bulletins/420b2fe)). Check the outcome before relying on it.

**Out-of-state firewood is banned** unless it has been heat-treated to 160°F for 75 minutes (USDA T314-c), labeled, and comes from a USDA- or state-certified facility. **Kiln-drying alone does not qualify** ([MFS FAQ](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/mfs/forest_health/invasive_threats/firewood_out_of_state_ban_faqs.html); [Firewood Board](https://firewood.nationalplantboard.org/?p=138)). This ban is separate from the ash borer quarantine, so it should still favor local wood for campgrounds and campers if the quarantine is repealed.

**Selling by the cord.** Firewood must be sold by the cord: 128 cu ft stacked, or 180 cu ft loose-thrown for 12–16 inch pieces. Terms like "truckload" are not allowed. DACF Weights and Measures handles complaints ([BDN](https://bangordailynews.com/2019/10/10/homestead/buyers-of-maine-firewood-do-have-options-when-they-feel-cheated/)).

**Reporting.** Dealers file Maine Forest Service wood-processor reports ([12 M.R.S. §8884, MFS report](https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Rpts/hd9757_m2m344_2011.pdf)).

## Risks

Labor-heavy, thin margins on cord wood, and it depends on the timber supply.

## How it fits

It sells bundles to the farm's own campsites and to other Midcoast campgrounds. Leftovers from a mobile sawmill become firewood.
