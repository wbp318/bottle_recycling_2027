# Firewood

**Rank 4.** Good if the farm has a woodlot. Quarantine rules give in-state wood an edge.

## Rules that matter

**Emerald ash borer quarantine (changed 2024–2026).** All of Kennebec, Knox, Lincoln and Waldo counties are inside the regulated area, which restricts moving hardwood firewood and ash out of it. The area was expanded by emergency order through 2025, and a new map came out in April 2026 ([MFS regulated areas, April 2026](https://www1.maine.gov/dacf/mfs/forest_health/invasive_threats/eab/documents/Maine%20EAB%20Infested%20regulated%20Areas%20April%202026.pdf); [maine.gov/eab](https://maine.gov/eab)).

**Out-of-state firewood is banned** unless it has been heat-treated to 160°F for 75 minutes (USDA T314-c), labeled, and comes from a USDA- or state-certified facility. **Kiln-drying alone does not qualify** ([MFS FAQ](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/mfs/forest_health/invasive_threats/firewood_out_of_state_ban_faqs.html); [Firewood Board](https://firewood.nationalplantboard.org/?p=138)). That makes local wood the default for campgrounds and campers.

**Selling by the cord.** Firewood must be sold by the cord: 128 cu ft stacked, or 180 cu ft loose-thrown for 12–16 inch pieces. Terms like "truckload" are not allowed. DACF Weights and Measures handles complaints ([BDN](https://bangordailynews.com/2019/10/10/homestead/buyers-of-maine-firewood-do-have-options-when-they-feel-cheated/)).

**Reporting.** Dealers file Maine Forest Service wood-processor reports ([12 M.R.S. §8884, MFS report](https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Rpts/hd9757_m2m344_2011.pdf)).

## Risks

Labor-heavy, thin margins on cord wood, and it depends on the timber supply.

## How it fits

It sells bundles to the farm's own campsites and to other Midcoast campgrounds. Leftovers from a mobile sawmill become firewood.
