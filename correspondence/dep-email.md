Hello,

My partner and I are planning to open a licensed beverage container redemption center in Maine in 2027. My partner lives in Maine and would manage the center day to day. The likely site is an outbuilding on his farm in [town], [county] County. We intend to apply as a Maine LLC and want to build the business around current program requirements before we file. I've grouped our questions below and am glad to set up a call if that's easier.

**Licensing and siting**

1. Could you send the current list of licensed redemption centers, with town, so we can see where coverage was lost after the recent closures?
2. For a municipality of 5,000 or fewer residents, is the limit one redemption center under 06-096 CMR ch. 426? Is [town] currently at its limit under 38 M.R.S. §3113(3)?
3. If a town is at its limit, what evidence does the Department accept as proof of compelling public need? For example, distance to the nearest center, population served, letters from dealers, or the hours of existing centers.
4. Are there towns or regions where the Department has heard from residents or dealers that no convenient center exists?
5. Are there siting or facility requirements beyond the application form? For example, minimum floor space, restroom, storage, parking, signage, or minimum hours. Does a site on agricultural property raise any particular issues?
6. Roughly how long does review of an initial application take once it's complete? How often are public hearings requested?

**Bag drop-off locations**

7. The application allows drop-off locations where customers leave bags and are paid later. If one of those locations is in a different town from the licensed center, does it count toward that town's limit? Does it need its own license?
8. What records does the Department expect for bag drop-offs: customer accounts, tags, payment timing?

**Dealer agreements**

9. Is the model dealer agreement on the Department's website current?
10. For a member dealer agreement with a retailer of 5,000 square feet or more, is there anything we should know about how the Department reviews those?

**Handling fee and the cooperative**

11. What is the current handling fee per container after the inflation adjustment under 38 M.R.S. §3106? We have 6 cents as of September 2023, indexed from January 2025.
12. Now that full commingling began October 1, 2026, what streams does a new center have to separate (material, deposit tier, size)? How do we register with the cooperative for pickup, and how is pickup scheduled for a new licensee?
13. Is it correct that centers using reverse vending machines or bulk processing don't need to sort glass by color?

**Out-of-state containers**

14. We understand the center may accept any container with a Maine label registered under §3105, but may not accept containers purchased outside Maine, and that the fine is $100 per container. Could you point us to the current statutory text and confirm the penalty?
15. For a person redeeming more than 2,500 containers at once, what exactly must the center record (name, address, license plate, count, date)? How long must we keep those records, and does the Department inspect them?
16. How does the Department expect a center to judge whether containers were purchased out of state, and what does it consider "knowingly" accepting them? We plan to:
    - require written agreements from commercial accounts
    - require registered accounts for bag drop-off customers
    - keep a log of large returns

    Is there anything else you'd recommend?

**Cost and Carbon Efficient Technology Fund**

17. When will the next CCET Fund solicitation (38 M.R.S. §3114-A) open?
18. Can a center with a pending license apply, or only a licensed one?
19. Does the fund cover leased reverse vending machines and installation costs such as electrical work and internet connection? Is there a typical award size now that the annual amount is $500,000?

**Other programs**

20. Is a fall 2026 round of the Solid Waste Diversion Grant planned? We'd consider a non-deposit recycling or reuse drop-off at the site. If that's handled by Mark King's program instead, I'm happy to contact him directly.

Thank you for your time. We'd like to get this right before we file.
