## The top five

**Black Bear Consulting Corps.**

- **What it is:** a small team of vetted students works on business projects for five weeks: market research, competitive analysis, web design, copywriting, design, or coding.
- **Cost:** free for companies under 50 employees.
- **Timing:** the fall 2026 cohort had a deadline of August 7 with rolling applications after that, and projects start early September or mid-to-late October. Ask about the late-October slot now; otherwise apply in early summer 2027.
- **Contact:** Emma Wilson, emma.wilson@maine.edu, 207-581-1454.
- **Good first project:** a market and site study, or a competitive scan of redemption centers.

**UMaine Computer Science Capstone (COS 397, then COS 497).**

- **How it runs:** students propose the project in fall (COS 397, taught by Laura Gurney in fall 2026) and build it in spring.
- **Fall 2027:** pitch between May and mid-August 2027.
- **Spring 2027:** teams formed this fall, but email now in case a team still needs a client.
- **Contact:** Director Penny Rheingans, penny.rheingans@maine.edu, 207-581-2183, or scis@maine.edu.
- **Good fit:** the counting dashboard, the booking and billing app, or route optimization.

**UMA Computer Information Systems internship.**

- **Why it fits:** Augusta is in Kennebec County, so it's likely the closest campus.
- **What it is:** required for the bachelor's degree. A three-way partnership between the student, a faculty mentor, and us, built around an IT project. Pay can be hourly or a project stipend.
- **Remote:** UMA is largely online, which fits a remote supervisor.
- **Contacts by track:**
  - Programming and data science: Matthew Dube, 207-621-3212
  - Network and security: Henry Felch, 207-621-3371
  - Web: Diana Kokoska, 207-262-7864
- **Timing:** contact Dube now about spring 2027; post summer roles by February or March 2027.

**Innovate for Maine Fellows.** A paid summer fellowship placing Maine college students with growing, innovating Maine for-profit companies. Pitch the software and computer-vision angle. Budget about $18–22/hr for 10 weeks, roughly $7–9k (est.). Contact the Foster Center between October and December 2026 for summer 2027.

**USM Career Exploration.**

- **What it is:** paid semester internships at $19.50/hr, 10–20 hours a week, at least 120 hours.
- **Who pays:** the page presents it as a USM program, so the wage may be covered. **Ask who pays.**
- **Timing:** spring 2027 matching is already done (internships run Jan 25 to May 7, 2027). Ask to be listed for summer or fall 2027.
- **Contact:** Lindsey Freeman, lindsey.w.freeman@maine.edu, or hubinterns@maine.edu.

## Who owns the code

No sponsored-capstone IP policy was published for UMaine SCIS, ECE, or ME. By default, students own the code they write unless they assign it, and universities often won't allow NDAs or exclusive assignments for graded work. Before a project starts, either:

- agree that the code is released under an open-source license in a public repo the students can show in their portfolios, or
- get a signed assignment to the LLC, if the department allows it.

For paid interns, put an IP assignment in the offer letter.

## Not a student pipeline

- **MaineSpark** (Maine Development Foundation) promotes credential goals and doesn't place interns.
- **Maine Career Exploration**, the statewide one, places **high school** students. USM's similarly named program is the college one.
- **Maine Technology Institute** has no intern program. It offers consultants (Maine Entrepreneurial Resource Corps) and innovation grants.
